# Roster bible: 20 fighters

The content source of truth for every fighter: who they are, how they play, their moves and their lines. Read it together with the content rules in [PRD §8](./PRD.md#8-content-legal-and-ethics-guidelines-must-have). **Every name, move and line here is a placeholder until it passes that review.**

## How to read this

**Every fighter has the same kit:**

| Slot | Touch input | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 6 normals | Light / Heavy, standing, crouching or jumping | Mostly **shared animations per body rig** (see PRD §5.2). Props and hit effects are per fighter |
| Throw | Throw button | Unique throw animation and gag |
| Special 1 | **S** | |
| Special 2 | **→ + S** (forward + Special) | |
| Special 3 | **← + S** (back + Special) | |
| Super | Tap **Super** (costs 1 bar) | Unique animation |
| Jalsa Ultimate | Hold **Super** (costs 3 bars) | **Comic-panel cinematic**: 2–4 illustrated panels with zoom, shake and a voice line. This is the clip-worthy moment |
| Gimmick | — | One unique rule per fighter. **This is what makes each fighter feel different** |
| Taunt | Taunt button | Voice line plus a small meter gain. Can be punished |
| Lines | — | Intro, taunt, win, lose, plus rival-specific intros (see the end of this file) |

Lines are written in **Roman Urdu**, with English translations for the subtitles and the English language option.

**Body rigs** (shared animation skeletons): **A** slim · **B** medium · **C** large · **D** compact · **U** unique

**Party balance:** *Tehreek-e-Tabdeeli* 4 · *PML-Nihari* 4 · *Awami Party Zindabad* 3 · *Muttahida Patang Front* 2 · single-fighter parties 3 · non-politicians 4. All four non-politicians mock *the system* equally, with no party bias.

## Tehreek-e-Tabdeeli (PTT)
*Parody colours: green and red with a cricket-ball emblem.*

### 1. Kaptaan
**Archetype:** cricketer-turned-politician. Charismatic, sporty, famous for changing direction. **Style:** all-rounder, the "learn the game with me" fighter.
**Gimmick: U-Turn.** During any special, press ← to U-turn. He spins 180°, cancels the move and faces the other way. It's great for mix-ups, and the announcer shouts *"U-TURN!"*

| Input | Move | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| S | **Bouncer** | Fast cricket-ball projectile that bounces once off the floor |
| → S | **U-Turn Dash** | Forward dash. Press S again mid-dash to reverse and cross up |
| ← S | **Reverse Swing** | Rising bat-swing anti-air |
| Super | **Container Speech** | Climbs a shipping container. Speech sound waves stun across the screen |
| Ultimate | **'92 Ka Hero** | Comic panels: run-up, delivery, stumps exploding, opponent "BOWLED!" |

**Lines.** Intro: *"Main inko rulaunga!"* (I'll make them cry!). Taunt: *"Ghabrana nahi hai!"* (Don't panic!). Win: *"Maine kaha tha na, ghabrana nahi hai."* (Told you, don't panic.) Lose: *"Yeh haar nahi, U-turn hai."* (That's not a loss, it's a U-turn.)
**Content note:** no jokes about jail, court cases or his family.

### 2. Mr. Plus
**Archetype:** the quiet provincial chief minister, hand-picked and famously praised as a world-class bowler "plus". **Style:** slow burn. He's weak early and a monster once powered up.
**Gimmick: Khamoshi (silence).** His meter fills passively while he isn't attacking. He never speaks: every voice line is a subtitle reading *"…"*.

| Input | Move | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| S | **Silent Approval** | Nods slowly. Counter stance that absorbs one hit and stamps back |
| → S | **File Approved** | Giant rubber-stamp slam (overhead) |
| ← S | **Inswinger** | Low skidding ball that curves toward the opponent |
| Super | **Plus Mode** | Glows. +20% speed and damage for 8 seconds |
| Ultimate | **Plus Plus** | Comic panels: bowls six deliveries at absurd speed, scoreboard reads "6 WICKETS, 0 WORDS" |

**Lines.** Intro: *"…"*. Taunt: (shrugs) *"…"*. Win: *"…"*, with a caption: *"Mr. Plus has issued a statement."* Lose: *"…"* (looks at his phone for instructions).

### 3. Doctor 19 Points
**Archetype:** outspoken former minister, remembered for explaining that Covid-19 "has 19 points". **Style:** zoner with lots of projectiles.
**Gimmick: 19 Points.** Each hit she lands adds a point to a counter. At **19 points**, her next Super is free and the counter resets.

| Input | Move | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| S | **Point Number One** | Throws a bullet-point projectile ("•1", "•2"…) |
| → S | **Science Lecture** | Chalkboard slides out. Chalk barrage, multi-hit |
| ← S | **Climate Change** | Small whirlwind anti-air |
| Super | **Covid ke 19 Points** | 19-hit list combo, each hit numbered on screen |
| Ultimate | **PhD Thesis** | Comic panels: a 400-page thesis titled "19 POINTS" drops on the opponent |

**Lines.** Intro: *"Is fight ke 19 points hain."* (This fight has 19 points.) Taunt: *"Point number 20 bhi hai!"* (There's a point 20 too!) Win: *"Science ne prove kar diya."* (Science has proven it.) Lose: *"Mujhe sirf 18 points yaad thay."* (I only remembered 18 points.)

### 4. March Wala
**Archetype:** fiery provincial chief minister who keeps announcing marches on the capital. **Style:** rushdown charger with big, committal moves.
**Gimmick: Ghayab! (vanished).** Once per round he can vanish completely for 1 second and reappear behind the opponent.

| Input | Move | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| S | **Mic Threat** | Shouts into a megaphone. Short-range shockwave |
| → S | **Lashkar March** | Charges with a crowd of marchers behind him. Armoured, multi-hit |
| ← S | **Ghayab!** | Vanish, reappear behind |
| Super | **Capital Chalo** | Leads a full march across the screen, containers falling everywhere |
| Ultimate | **Pahar Ka Rasta** | Comic panels: vanishes into the mountains, then lands on the opponent from a peak |

**Lines.** Intro: *"Hum aa rahe hain!"* (We're coming!). Taunt: *"Ab koi nahi rokega!"* (Nobody can stop us now!). Win: *"March kamyab!"* (March successful!) Lose: *"Main to bas… ghayab tha."* (I was just… missing.)
**Content note:** no references to detentions, arrests or real crackdowns.

## PML-Nihari

**Gimmick: London.** Once per round he leaves the screen for 3 seconds (invincible), then flies back in on any of 3 attack angles.

### 7. Madam CM
**Archetype:** the social-media-savvy provincial chief minister, always on camera. **Style:** trickster / zoner.

**Content note:** mock the politics and the PR, never her appearance, gender or family.

### 8. Sharm Sahib

## Muttahida Patang Front (MPF)
*Parody colours: red, white and green, with a kite emblem.*

### 12. Patang Bhai ⚠️
**Archetype:** the Karachi party boss who addresses rallies by phone from abroad, sometimes singing. **Style:** long-range puppet. A giant speakerphone on a stand does the fighting while he "calls in".
**Gimmick: Phone Line.** Attacks come from the speakerphone, which he can reposition. If the opponent breaks the speaker, he's vulnerable for 3 seconds.

| Input | Move | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| S | **Phone-In Address** | Delayed long-range sound wave |
| → S | **Patang Glide** | Rides a kite through the air |
| ← S | **Taraana** | Starts singing. The opponent sways and slows down |
| Super | **Strike Call** | The city shuts down and the opponent freezes for 2 seconds |
| Ultimate | **Nine-Hour Speech** | Comic panels: the clock spins and the opponent falls asleep |

**Lines.** Intro: *"Hello? Awaz aa rahi hai?"* (Hello? Can you hear me?) Taunt: (singing) *"Bhaiyo aur behno…"* (Brothers and sisters…). Win: *"Line kat gayi."* (The line dropped.) Lose: *"Signal nahi aa raha!"* (No signal!)
**⚠️ Legal note:** Pakistani courts have restricted media coverage of the real figure this parodies. **Get legal advice before designing this fighter.** Fallback: make Patang Bhai a generic "party boss on speakerphone" with no resemblance to that person, or swap in a bench fighter.

## Secret boss: The Hidden Hand ⚠️
Faceless and unnamed, with no uniform and no insignia. A hand in a shadowy sleeve, and occasionally an empty chair. **Unlocked by finishing Arcade mode.** **Requires legal review before any design work starts (PRD §8.8).**

## Who isn't on the roster, and why
- **Leaders of religious parties, and any religious figure** (PRD §8.5).
- **Politicians who were killed, and any reference to their deaths** (§8.6).
- **Family members who aren't in public life** (§8.4).
- **Serving military, judiciary or intelligence officials** (§8.8). The Hidden Hand is the only, faceless, exception, pending review.
