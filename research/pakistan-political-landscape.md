# Pakistan Political Landscape (as of 31 October 2026)

Background research for a satirical game whose characters will be caricatures of real public figures.

## How to read this file

- **Snapshot date:** 31 October 2026. Pakistani politics moves fast. Treat anything marked **[unverified]** as needing a fresh check before it goes into the game.
- **Method:** Built from web-search results (Dawn, Express Tribune, Geo, ARY, Aaj, Arab News, Al Jazeera, Reuters/AP syndication and similar). Direct page fetches were blocked in this environment (Wikipedia and Al Jazeera did not resolve), so 2026 facts rest on search-result summaries of dated news articles. Pre-2025 background comes from well-documented, widely reported events.
- **Legal language:** "Convicted" means a trial court verdict. Many convictions are under appeal, and several were later overturned. "Alleged" means not proven in court. Nothing here is rumour. Where outlets disagree, both versions are noted.
- **Satire caution:** The people below are real and mostly alive. Several are in jail or facing trial, and some have been accused of terrorism or sedition in proceedings that rights groups call unfair. Jokes about their health, imprisonment or family members carry more risk than jokes about public conduct, slogans and political U-turns.

### 1.2 Ruling coalition and opposition

- **Government side:** PML-N leads the federal cabinet. PPP is the indispensable partner. It holds the presidency, the Senate chair, the Sindh and Balochistan governments and the KP and Punjab governorships, but has mostly stayed out of the federal cabinet. An invitation to join was reported in June–July 2026, and no outcome has been confirmed. Other partners are MQM-P (education and health ministries), PML-Q, IPP (communications ministry), BAP, PML-Z and the National Party **[NP's current alignment unverified]**.
- **Strength:** Reserved seats were reallocated after the Supreme Court constitutional bench's review ruling of 27 June 2025, which denied PTI/SIC those seats. That gave the coalition a two-thirds majority (about 233–235 of 336 seats in July 2025). An August 2026 ARY analysis put the coalition at 244, with PML-N 132 and PPP 74 **[figures unverified, and the article had internal inconsistencies]**.
- **PPP–PML-N friction (2025–26):** The two parties clashed over Punjab flood-aid remarks (Maryam vs Bilawal, Oct 2025), Cholistan canals (2025), proposals to create new provinces, and the August 2026 AJK election, where PPP alleged rigging. In September 2026, PPP threatened to block legislation. It still backed the October 2026 KP emergency in Parliament.
- **Opposition:** PTI (its legislators sit as independents or under the SIC banner) is the main opposition. It works through **TTAP** (Tehreek-e-Tahaffuz-e-Ayeen-e-Pakistan, "Movement to Protect the Constitution of Pakistan"), an alliance led by Mahmood Achakzai that includes PTI, PkMAP, MWM and, at times, Awaam Pakistan figures. JUI-F, Jamaat-e-Islami, BNP-M, ANP and GDA are outside government but act separately from PTI. TTAP called off talks with the government on 15 Oct 2026.

### 1.3 The military establishment

- **"The establishment"** is the standard euphemism for the army high command and intelligence agencies (ISI, MI). Since 1947 they have ruled directly for about 33 years (Ayub, Yahya, Zia, Musharraf) and shaped civilian politics the rest of the time. No PM has completed a full five-year term.
- **Asim Munir's ascendancy (2022–26):**
  - April–May 2025 India crisis: the Pahalgam attack (22 April), India's "Operation Sindoor" (7 May), Pakistan's "Operation Bunyan-um-Marsoos" (10 May), and a ceasefire announced on 10 May. Pakistan brands the episode "Marka-e-Haq" (Battle for Truth).
  - 20 May 2025: Munir promoted to **Field Marshal**, only the second after Ayub Khan (1959).
  - June 2025: lunch with Donald Trump at the White House.
  - Sept 2025: Saudi–Pakistan Strategic Mutual Defence Agreement.
  - April 2026: Munir is credited alongside Shehbaz with brokering the US–Iran "Islamabad Talks".
- **27th Amendment (passed and signed 10–13 Nov 2025):**
  - Creates the post of **Chief of Defence Forces** (held by the COAS), placing the army, navy and air force under one command.
  - Abolishes the Chairman Joint Chiefs of Staff Committee.
  - Makes Field Marshal / Marshal of the Air Force / Admiral of the Fleet ranks lifelong, with legal protections (critics call it lifetime immunity).
  - Creates a **Federal Constitutional Court (FCC)** that takes most constitutional jurisdiction away from the Supreme Court.
  - Supreme Court Justices **Syed Mansoor Ali Shah** and **Athar Minallah** resigned in protest. A Lahore High Court judge also resigned.
  - Critics, PTI and many lawyers call it the constitutionalisation of military supremacy.
- **ISI:** Lt Gen Asim Malik holds both the ISI chief and NSA posts, the first serving DG ISI to do so. Former DG ISI **Faiz Hameed** was sentenced by court martial to 14 years in Dec 2025. This was the first conviction of an ex-spy chief, and it is framed as punishment for political meddling (in PTI's favour).
- **ISPR:** The military's media wing. DG ISPR **Lt Gen Ahmed Sharif Chaudhry** is a public face. On 5 Dec 2025 he called Imran Khan "mentally ill" and a "security risk" at a GHQ press conference.

### 1.4 Judiciary

- **26th Amendment (Oct 2024):**
  - A parliamentary committee now picks the CJP from the three senior-most judges. This bypassed Justice Mansoor Ali Shah, who was expected to become CJP by seniority.
  - Constitutional benches were created.
  - Reshaped the Judicial Commission.
- **27th Amendment (Nov 2025):** created the FCC, so the judiciary now has two apex courts. CJP Afridi ruled in May 2026 that the SC and FCC are "coordinate courts" with separate jurisdictions.
- **Recurring flashpoints:**
  - Reserved seats. The SC full court gave them to PTI in July 2024, and the constitutional bench reversed that in June 2025.
  - Military trials of civilians for May 9. A constitutional bench upheld them in May 2025.
  - Imran Khan's jail access and health. In August 2026 the SC ordered his transfer to Shifa International Hospital, and the government sought review.
  - The Islamabad High Court, where Chief Justice Sarfraz Dogar is hearing Imran and Bushra's £190m appeals.

### 1.5 Election Commission of Pakistan (ECP)

- CEC Sikandar Sultan Raja was appointed under Imran Khan in 2020, and PTI now calls him a partisan enemy.
- The ECP refused to accept PTI's intra-party elections. The Supreme Court upheld this on 13 Jan 2024, so PTI lost its **cricket-bat symbol** before the 2024 election. Its candidates ran as independents.
- After May 9 convictions, the ECP disqualified opposition lawmakers in bulk (Aug 2025 onward), including both opposition leaders, Omar Ayub (NA) and Shibli Faraz (Senate), and SIC chief Hamid Raza.
- As of Dec 2025 it did not recognise Barrister Gohar as PTI chairman.
- It delisted Shahid Khaqan Abbasi's Awaam Pakistan party (Aug 2026, under challenge).

### 1.6 The 8 February 2024 general election and its disputes

- **Run-up:**
  - Imran Khan had been jailed since August 2023.
  - Just before polling he was convicted in the cipher, Toshakhana and "iddat/nikah" cases. The cipher and iddat verdicts were later overturned on appeal.
  - PTI lost its bat symbol, so its candidates ran on random symbols.
  - Rallies were blocked, and candidates were arrested or forced to withdraw. Many PTI leaders had "press-conferenced" out of the party after May 9, 2023.
- **Polling day:** Mobile phone and data services were suspended nationwide, officially for security. Results were delayed by many hours, which fed rigging claims.
- **Results (general seats):**
  - PTI-backed independents won about 93, the largest bloc.
  - PML-N 75, PPP 54, MQM-P 17. The small remainder went to JUI-F, PML-Q, IPP and others.
  - PTI claimed it had won far more and that results were changed between **Form 45** (the polling-station count) and **Form 47** (the constituency consolidation).
  - Nawaz Sharif lost in Mansehra. His Lahore win over jailed Yasmin Rashid is one of PTI's showcase "Form 47" disputes.
- **Rawalpindi commissioner:** On 17 Feb 2024, Liaqat Ali Chattha publicly confessed to manipulating results in his division and then retracted. It became a meme ("Chattha confession").
- **Government formation:** PML-N and PPP formed a government with MQM-P and others. PTI-backed MNAs joined the **Sunni Ittehad Council (SIC)** to claim reserved seats, a legal saga that ended with PTI denied (June 2025).
- **Observers:** FAFEN, the EU, the US State Department and the UK raised concerns about the fairness of the process. The ECP and government reject rigging claims.

## 2. Parties

**About seat counts:** The National Assembly has 336 seats: 266 general, 60 women's and 10 minority reserved seats. Party totals below are approximate and **[unverified]** unless stated. Disqualifications and by-elections keep changing PTI-bloc numbers, and no official late-2026 party-position table could be retrieved. Electoral symbols marked [unverified] could not be confirmed.

### 2.1 Pakistan Muslim League (Nawaz) – PML-N
- **History:** Grew out of Zia-era Muslim League factions. Nawaz Sharif led it to power in 1990, 1997 and 2013. Musharraf's 1999 coup sent the family into exile. It led the 2022 PDM coalition that ousted Imran Khan, and has led the federal and Punjab governments since 2024.
- **Ideology:** Centre-right, pro-business, "development" politics (motorways, metro buses, power plants). Mildly conservative. Rhetorically pro-civilian supremacy in 2017–20 ("vote ko izzat do"), but since 2022 it is widely seen as the establishment's preferred civilian partner (the "hybrid" setup).
- **Base:** Central and northern Punjab (Lahore, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Sialkot, Rawalpindi), Hazara and the urban trading class.
- **Symbol/colours:** **Tiger (sher)**, green flag. Supporters roar "Sher! Sher!" and bring live or stuffed tigers to rallies.
- **NA strength:** About 132 including reserved seats (ARY, Aug 2026) **[unverified]**. 2024 general seats: 75.
- **Alliance:** Leads the federal government. Holds Punjab.
- **Slogans and imagery:**
  - "Vote ko izzat do" (Respect the vote).
  - "Dekho dekho kaun aya, sher aya sher aya".
  - "Mujhe kyun nikala?" (Nawaz, 2017, now a mocking meme).
  - Detractors call supporters "patwaris" (land-record clerks).
  - Family dynasty: Nawaz, Shehbaz, Maryam, Hamza, plus in-law Ishaq Dar.

### 2.2 Pakistan Peoples Party – PPP (electoral wing: PPP-Parliamentarians, PPPP)
- **History:** Founded in 1967 by Zulfikar Ali Bhutto, who was hanged under Zia in 1979. Benazir Bhutto was PM twice and was assassinated in 2007. Asif Ali Zardari has run it since, and Bilawal Bhutto Zardari has been chairman since 2007.
- **Ideology:** Nominally centre-left and social-democratic ("Islam is our faith, democracy is our politics, socialism is our economy"). In practice a patronage machine in Sindh. Pro-18th Amendment provincial autonomy.
- **Base:** Rural and urban Sindh (rules Sindh uninterrupted since 2008), southern Punjab pockets, and some of Balochistan (Bugti government).
- **Symbol/colours:** **Arrow (teer)**. Red–black–green tricolour.
- **NA strength:** About 74 (2024 general seats: 54).
- **Alliance:** Key coalition partner. It holds the presidency, the Senate chair, two provinces and two governorships, but mostly stays out of the federal cabinet. It fought PML-N over flood aid, canals, new provinces and AJK in 2025–26.
- **Slogans and imagery:**
  - "Roti, kapra aur makaan" (bread, clothes, shelter).
  - "Zinda hai Bhutto, zinda hai" (Bhutto lives).
  - "Jeay Bhutto".
  - "Ek Zardari sab pe bhari" (One Zardari outweighs everyone).
  - "Democracy is the best revenge" (Benazir's line, quoted by Zardari and Bilawal).
  - Workers are called "jiyalas".
  - Imagery: portraits of ZAB and Benazir, Garhi Khuda Bakhsh mausoleum gatherings on death anniversaries.

### 2.3 Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf – PTI
- **History:** Founded in 1996 by Imran Khan. It surged from 2011 and ran KP from 2013 until the October 2026 suspension. It won the 2018 election (opponents say with establishment help), and Khan was ousted by a no-confidence vote in April 2022. After **May 9, 2023** it faced a crackdown: mass defections, arrests, loss of its symbol and military trials.
- **Ideology:** Populist and anti-corruption ("Naya Pakistan", "Riyasat-e-Madina" welfare-state rhetoric). Personality-centred around Khan. Since 2022, sharply anti-establishment and anti-US-interference ("Haqeeqi Azadi", real freedom).
- **Base:** KP (dominant), urban Punjab youth, the diaspora (UK, US, Gulf) and the social-media generation.
- **Symbol/colours:** **Cricket bat** (revoked Jan 2024). Red and green flag. In 2024 its candidates ran on random symbols, a running joke **[specific symbols unverified]**.
- **NA strength:** About 93 PTI-backed winners in 2024. Now fewer after May 9 disqualifications. They sit as independents or under the SIC label **[current number unverified]**.
- **Alliance:** Main opposition. Leads TTAP with Achakzai's PkMAP and MWM. Its KP government is suspended.
- **Leadership structure (late 2026):**
  - Founder: Imran Khan (jailed).
  - Chairman: Barrister Gohar Ali Khan (ECP disputes recognition).
  - Secretary-general: Salman Akram Raja (offered resignation in 2026, rejected **[unverified]**).
  - President: Pervaiz Elahi **[unverified]**.
  - Vice-chairman: Shah Mahmood Qureshi (jailed).
  - KP president: Junaid Akbar (in custody).
  - International spokesperson: Zulfi Bukhari.
  - Family influence: sisters Aleema, Uzma and Noreen, and wife Bushra.
- **Slogans and imagery:**
  - "Tabdeeli aa gayi hai" (change has come).
  - "Ghabrana nahi hai" (don't panic).
  - "Imported hukumat namanzoor" (imported government unacceptable, 2022).
  - "Absolutely not".
  - "Qaidi No. 804" (Imran's prisoner number, used as a hashtag and on merch).
  - "Imran Khan ko riha karo" (Free Imran Khan).
  - Imagery: containers, DJ Butt's music at rallies, the green-red flag, slogan-heavy TikTok and X campaigns.
  - Critics call supporters "youthias" or "PTI trolls". Supporters call the government "PDM" or "Form 47 government".

#### PTI offshoots and splinters
- **Istehkam-e-Pakistan Party (IPP):**
  - Founded June 2023 by **Jahangir Tareen** with about 100 ex-PTI defectors after May 9. Widely described as an establishment-midwifed "King's party" for PTI defectors.
  - President: **Abdul Aleem Khan** (federal communications minister).
  - Won only a handful of NA seats in 2024 (Aleem Khan NA-117, Awn Chaudhry NA-128) **[exact count unverified]**.
  - In government. Contested the 2026 AJK election. Pushes "16 provinces".
  - Symbol: eagle **[unverified]**.
- **PTI-Parliamentarians (PTI-P):**
  - Founded July 2023 by ex-KP CM **Pervez Khattak**, with ex-PTI figures such as Mahmood Khan.
  - Wiped out in KP in 2024. Largely defunct as an electoral force.
  - Khattak's later roles **[unverified]**.
- **Sunni Ittehad Council (SIC):** See 2.18. Used as PTI's parliamentary vehicle.
- **Ex-PTI dissidents without a party:** Fawad Chaudhry, Mahmood Moulvi, Imran Ismail and others. In late 2025–26 Fawad promoted a "National Dialogue Committee" of ex-PTI moderates, which Qureshi rejected (Jan 2026).

### 2.4 Muttahida Qaumi Movement – Pakistan (MQM-P)
- **History:**
  - Altaf Hussain founded APMSO in 1978 and the Mohajir Qaumi Movement in 1984. It was renamed "Muttahida" in 1997.
  - It dominated Karachi and Hyderabad for three decades, alleged to use militant enforcement. Army operations targeted it in 1992 and 2013–16.
  - After Altaf's anti-Pakistan speech on 22 Aug 2016, the Karachi leadership broke away as MQM-P.
  - Mustafa Kamal's PSP merged back in Jan 2023.
- **Ideology:** Ethnic Urdu-speaking (Mohajir) urban middle-class party. Secular-leaning. Demands local-government powers and more Karachi seats.
- **Base:** Karachi and Hyderabad.
- **Symbol/colours:** **Kite (patang)**. Red–white–green tricolour.
- **NA strength:** 17 general seats in 2024, about 22 with reserved seats **[unverified]**.
- **Alliance:** In federal government (Education: Khalid Maqbool Siddiqui; Health: Mustafa Kamal). Holds the Sindh governorship (Kamran Tessori). Opposition in Sindh against PPP.
- **Internal politics:** In 2026 a "Bahadurabad" camp (Siddiqui, Farooq Sattar, Tessori) and Mustafa Kamal's camp held rival rallies. They paper over the split in joint press conferences.
- **Imagery:**
  - Old HQ "Nine Zero" (Azizabad).
  - Slogan "Hum ko manzil nahin, rehnuma chahiye" (We don't want the destination, we want the leader), a relic of the Altaf era.
  - March 2026 jibe: "Winner of 170 seats in jail and 80 seats in govt", MQM-P's quip about the 2024 election **[context unverified]**.

### 2.5 MQM-London (MQM-L)
- **History:** Altaf Hussain's rump faction since the 2016 split, run from London (Edgware).
- **Status:** Altaf's speeches have been barred from Pakistani media since a 2015 Lahore High Court order. It effectively cannot organise in Pakistan, and it boycotts or is excluded from elections.
- **Imagery:** Altaf's marathon phone-in speeches, songs and tears. Supporters' chant "Jeay Altaf". Recurring hospitalisation news in 2025–26.

### 2.6 Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam (Fazl) – JUI-F
- **History:** Deobandi clerical party descended from the 1945 JUI. It has been led by Maulana Fazlur Rehman since 1980 (originally with his father Mufti Mahmud's legacy). It has been in almost every government since 1988, as the ultimate swing party.
- **Ideology:** Deobandi Islamist and seminary-based. Pragmatic dealmaker. Anti-PTI historically, but since 2024 it has opposed the PML-N/PPP government too and demands new elections.
- **Base:** Pashtun belt of KP (D.I. Khan, Bannu, Lakki), Pashtun north Balochistan (Pishin, Zhob) and madrassa networks.
- **Symbol/colours:** **Book (kitab)**. Black-and-white striped flag.
- **NA strength:** About 4 general seats in 2024 (about 10 with reserved seats) **[unverified]**.

## 4. Recurring themes and memes in Pakistani political culture

| Theme / phrase | What it means | Satire hooks |
|---|---|---|
| **"The establishment" / "Pindi" / "the powers that be"** | Army high command and intelligence agencies, headquartered in Rawalpindi (GHQ). Also "boots", "uniform", "angels" (*farishtay*), "**khalai makhlooq**" (aliens, for unidentified agency men), and "**namaloom afraad**" (unknown persons) who pick people up. | An unseen faction that controls the game's "real" levers. Characters ask "has Pindi signed off?" |
| **Selected vs Elected** | Bilawal's 2018 jibe at Imran as a "selected" PM installed by the army. Now flipped by PTI against the "Form 47" government. | Every PM is accused of being "selected" by the last one. |
| **"Same page"** | PTI-era boast (2018–21) that government and army were "on the same page". Now applied to Shehbaz–Munir "hybrid" rule. | A literal page everyone fights to stand on. |
| **"Neutral"** | In 2022 the army declared itself "neutral". Imran replied "neutral to janwar hota hai" (only an animal is neutral). | Neutrality as a costume the army changes. |
| **Gate No. 4** | GHQ's gate. Sheikh Rashid called himself its product. Shorthand for establishment-made politicians. | A literal gate politicians queue at. |
| **King's party** | Parties assembled for a ruler or the establishment: PML-Q (Musharraf), BAP (2018), IPP (2023). Defectors "press-conference" their way out after May 9. | "Press conference" as a defection ritual, with the same script read by different people. |
| **Panama Papers / Iqama / Qatari letter / Calibri** | The 2016–17 saga that disqualified Nawaz: London flats, a Qatari prince's letter vouching for the Sharifs' money trail, a "trust deed" set in an anachronistic font, and an undeclared Dubai work permit. | "Mujhe kyun nikala?" as a sad-music cue. Fonts as forensic evidence. |
| **Mr Ten Percent / fake accounts** | Zardari-era corruption allegations. | Kickback meter. |
| **Cipher** | Imran waved a letter at a March 2022 rally claiming a US conspiracy. "Absolutely not", "Hum koi ghulam hain?" ("are we slaves?"). He was convicted and then acquitted over it. | A MacGuffin document that keeps changing meaning. |
| **Toshakhana** | The state gift depository. Imran sold gifts such as the Graff watch and a Bulgari set (convicted). Other PMs' gift lists were also embarrassing when published. | Gift vault heist minigame. |
| **May 9 (2023)** | Riots after Imran's arrest. Corps Commander's House ("Jinnah House") in Lahore torched, GHQ gate attacked. Followed by military trials, mass ATC convictions and disqualifications (2025–26). The state calls it "Black Day". PTI says it was a "false flag". | The defining fork in the timeline. |
| **Form 45 vs Form 47** | Form 45 is the polling-station result. Form 47 is the provisional consolidated result. PTI alleges results were changed between them in 2024. "Form 47 government" is the insult. | Two forms that never match. |
| **Bat symbol / "baingan"** | PTI lost its cricket bat symbol (Jan 2024). Candidates got assorted symbols, which became a meme **[specific symbols unverified]**. | Random-symbol roulette. |
| **Dharna / long march / container** | Sit-ins and marches on Islamabad: Imran's 126-day 2014 dharna on a container, TLP's Faizabad 2017, Fazl's Azadi March 2019, PTI's Nov 2024 "final call" and Oct 2026 march. The state answers with **shipping containers** blocking every road. | Container-maze level. "Final call" that is never final. |
| **D-Chowk** | Islamabad's protest square in front of Parliament. | Boss arena. |
| **U-turn** | Imran's 2018 claim that "a leader who doesn't take U-turns isn't a leader". Every party now accuses the other of U-turns (Shehbaz on Zardari, Zardari on the army, etc.). | Steering-wheel collectible. |
| **"Ghabrana nahi hai"** | Imran's "Don't panic" refrain amid inflation. | Morale meter that never helps. |
| **"Tabdeeli", "Naya Pakistan", "Riyasat-e-Madina", "50 lakh ghar, 1 crore naukriyan"** | PTI's 2018 promises. | Unfulfilled-promise inventory. |
| **NRO** | National Reconciliation Ordinance (2007 Musharraf amnesty). Imran's catchphrase: "I won't give anyone an NRO". The NAB amendments of 2022 are called "NRO-2". | Get-out-of-jail card. |
| **"Vote ko izzat do"** | PML-N's 2018 slogan (respect the vote), now thrown back at it. | |
| **"Ek Zardari sab pe bhari"**, "**Zinda hai Bhutto**", "**Roti kapra makaan**" | PPP's eternal slogans. | Ghost of Bhutto as a narrator. |
| **Load-shedding, circular debt, IPPs, electricity bills** | Scheduled power cuts. Unpaid energy-sector debt. Capacity payments to private power producers drive huge bills. JI burns bills on stage. | Lights going out mid-speech. |
| **IMF** | Pakistan has been in about 24 IMF programmes. Each government promises it is the "last programme". The current $7bn EFF runs to 2027. | IMF lady arriving with a checklist. "Staff-level agreement" as a boss phase. |
| **Petrol price night announcements** | Fortnightly fuel-price changes, announced late at night. Huge swings in 2026 (Iran war). | Midnight price roulette. |
| **Atta (flour) and sugar crises** | Periodic shortages blamed on hoarders and "sugar mafias" (Tareen inquiry 2020). Shehbaz's "sell my clothes for cheap flour". | |
| **Missing persons / enforced disappearances** | Baloch and Pashtun activists, journalists, PTI workers "picked up" by "unknown persons". | Handle with care. A darker theme. |
| **Patwari / Youthia / Jiyala / "Fitna"** | Partisan labels: PML-N fans are "patwaris", PTI fans are "youthias", PPP workers are "jiyalas". PML-N and the state call PTI a "fitna" (sedition/mischief). | Faction names for supporters. |
| **"Lifafa journalist"** | "Envelope journalist", a journalist paid off by someone. Used by every party against hostile media. | |
| **Field Marshal / "Marka-e-Haq"** | Munir's 2025 elevation, the first since Ayub Khan. Wartime nationalism. Billboards. Trump's praise. | Statue-building minigame. History rhyming with Ayub Khan (and his grandson Omar Ayub as fugitive). |
| **Amendments as weapons** | The 18th (devolution, 2010), 26th (judiciary, 2024), 27th (CDF and FCC, 2025), and a possible 28th (rolling back devolution, 2026). Passed overnight, with "missing" senators and switching votes. | Late-night amendment speed-run. |
| **Governor's rule / emergency** | The October 2026 KP takeover. The governor from the rival party runs the province, and the CM is in "preventive custody". | Switching levels' ownership. |
| **Jail visits at Adiala** | Tuesday and Thursday meeting days. Sisters denied entry and staging sit-ins. Court orders on access to doctors, phone calls with sons, "chicken, mutton, milk, honey" facilities reports filed in the SC (Feb 2026). | Adiala gate as a recurring set. |
| **Honey** | Gandapur's 2016 "it was honey" (shahad) bottle. Sharjeel Memon's 2018 "honey and olive oil" hospital bottles. Honey also listed in Imran's jail-facilities report. | A cross-party honey running gag. |
| **Hybrid regime** | Analysts' term for civilian government fronting military power. Used for both the Imran (2018–22) and Shehbaz (2022–) eras. | |
| **Afghanistan and the "strategic depth" boomerang** | Faiz Hameed's 2021 Kabul tea moment, then the 2025–26 war with the Taliban government that Pakistan had once welcomed. | The tea cup as a cursed item. |
| **Mediator diplomacy** | Pakistan as host of the US–Iran Islamabad Talks (2026) and Trump's Nobel nominator, while fuel queues grow at home. | "Global peacemaker, domestic jailer" irony. |
