# Character Copy: Satirical Roster

Comedy copy for the Pakistani-politics satire game. Factual base: `research/pakistan-political-landscape.md` (snapshot 31 Oct 2026). Every character is a caricature of a real public figure under a parody name. The "Based on" line is for the design team only and should not appear in the shipped UI.

Contents:
1. Tone guide
2. Factions (17)
3. Character sheets (47 sheets, 49 people, because two sheets are duos)
4. Ensemble bits (15)
5. Exclusions and sensitivity notes

---

## 1. Tone guide

**The voice.** Spitting Image's grotesques, BNN's deadpan, and the weary four-panel cynicism of a Dawn editorial cartoon. The narrator is a tired late-night anchor. They have sat through every "final call", every "last IMF programme" and every "historic" amendment, and the only thing that would still surprise them is a politician keeping a promise. The contempt is affectionate and shared out equally. Nobody gets to be the good guys.

**Comic engines**, most useful first:
1. **The hypocrisy loop.** He said A, then he did B. Shehbaz promised to drag Zardari through the streets and ended up dragging him into the presidency.
2. **Literal jargon.** "Same Page" is an actual page, "Gate No. 4" is an actual gate, "neutral" is a gear-stick, and containers are a level mechanic.
3. **A real moment becomes a mechanic.** Every signature move comes from a documented public moment, so the joke also teaches the history.
4. **Institutional absurdity.** Two apex courts. A chief election commissioner whose term has ended. A governor running the province of the party that kept beating him.

**In bounds:** public conduct, speeches, slogans, U-turns, documented gaffes, viral moments, accurately described convictions and acquittals, the gap between rhetoric and record, and institutional power (military, judiciary, ECP, cabinet) with all its machinery.

**Out of bounds:**
- Ethnicity, sect, religion. That covers religious titles, scripture, prayer, religious dress, shrines and spiritual practice.
- Gender, body and appearance, apart from the signature props a person chose for themselves.
- Disability, illness, health and age.
- Family members who aren't public figures.
- Sexual content.
- Real violence and casualties, and enforced disappearances.
- Allegations stated as fact.

**Legal house style for the narrator:** use "convicted (appeal pending)", "acquitted", "later overturned", "alleged", "opponents call him" and "reportedly". The narrator never says "corrupt", "rigged" or "stole" in its own voice. Characters can accuse each other, because that's politics, but the narrator never confirms the accusation.

**Mixing Urdu and English:**
- UI, bios and mechanics are in English.
- Catchphrases, taunts and lines Pakistanis would quote word for word are in Roman Urdu.
- The English gloss goes in round brackets and the stage directions in square brackets.
- Spelling should be simple and consistent: *nahin, hai, hoon, kyun, karo*.
- Never mock an accent or how well someone speaks Urdu or English. Quote the specific public line instead. Bilawal's rain quote is fair game because it is that exact quote.
- Regional flavour comes only from well-known phrases ("Mitti pao", "Sher aya"), never from funny-accent spellings.

**The punch-up test.** Before a line ships, ask whether it targets the person's power, conduct or words. If it targets their body, faith, family or health, or the fact that they are behind bars, cut it.

## 2. Factions

Party symbols in this game are parodies. Real ECP-allotted symbols and flag designs should not be reproduced exactly.

### PML-Nihari (Pakistan Muslim League – Nihari)
- **Based on:** PML-N
- **Symbol:** The Sher (tiger), now on a long leash held by a khaki hand just off-screen
- **Colours:** Lion green, with breakfast-tray brass as the accent
- **Slogan:** "Vote ko izzat do" (Respect the vote) *[T&Cs apply]*
- **Supporter unit:** Patwaris
- **Blurb:** A Lahore family firm that has run Punjab, on and off, since the cassette era. In 2017 it campaigned on civilian supremacy. Since 2022 it has been the establishment's favourite civilian tenant, and it now governs the centre and Punjab. Its party tree has one trunk and every branch is a relative or an in-law. It builds motorways at great speed and cannot complete a five-year term.

### Pakistan Pedigree Party (PPP)
- **Based on:** Pakistan Peoples Party
- **Symbol:** The Teer (arrow), which can only fly inside Sindh's borders
- **Colours:** Red, black and green tricolour
- **Slogan:** "Roti, kapra aur makaan" (Bread, clothes and shelter) *[estimated delivery: 3 to 5 decades]*
- **Supporter unit:** Jiyalas
- **Blurb:** Founded by a man who promised bread, clothes and a roof, and run ever since by the people who inherited the promise. It has governed Sindh without a break since 2008. It holds the presidency, the Senate chair and two governorships, yet refuses to sit in the federal cabinet. It votes with the government, threatens to stop, and then votes with the government again.

### Pakistan Tehreek-e-Intezaar (PTI, Movement of Waiting)
- **Based on:** Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf
- **Symbol:** A cricket bat in a sealed evidence bag. Each round, PTI candidates draw a random replacement symbol
- **Colours:** Red and green, with container grey
- **Slogan:** "Ghabrana nahi hai" (Don't panic) and "Final call!" *[not final]*
- **Supporter unit:** Youthias (they respawn instantly on social media)
- **Blurb:**
  - Founded on Tabdeeli (change), Naya Pakistan and a captain's charisma.
  - It won in 2018, lost power to a no-confidence vote in 2022, and has spent the time since marching, petitioning and waiting outside Adiala's gate.
  - Its leader is inside and its chairman is unrecognised. Its province is under governor's rule, and its symbol is in a bag.
  - It is still the biggest bloc in the 2024 count, depending on which form you read.

### MQM-Partially (MQM-P)
- **Based on:** Muttahida Qaumi Movement – Pakistan
- **Symbol:** Two kites (patang) with their strings hopelessly tangled
- **Colours:** Red, white and green
- **Slogan:** "Hum ko manzil nahin, rehnuma chahiye" (We don't want the destination, we want the leader) *[just one this time, please]*
- **Blurb:** The Karachi party that split from its founder in 2016 and has been splitting from itself ever since. In 2026 the Bahadurabad camp and the Liaquatabad camp hold rival rallies, then hold a joint press conference to deny that there are two camps. It holds the education ministry, the health ministry and the Sindh governorship, and it is united against PPP and nothing else.

### London Office (MQM-Landline)
- **Based on:** MQM-London
- **Symbol:** A rotary telephone with an impossibly long cord
- **Colours:** Drizzle grey and phone-box red
- **Slogan:** "Jeay Bhai!" (Long live Bhai!) *[your call is important to us, please hold]*
- **Blurb:** Run from London since the 2016 split. It cannot organise in Pakistan, cannot appear on Pakistani media, and cannot stop talking. The faction has one leader, one phone line and an unlimited call plan.

### JUI-Flexi
- **Based on:** JUI-F (Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam, Fazl)
- **Symbol:** A weathervane. *Design note: replaces the party's real "book" symbol so that no book-shaped prop is ever mocked.*
- **Colours:** Black-and-white stripes
- **Slogan:** "Hum har haal mein jamhooriyat ke saath hain" (We stand with democracy in all circumstances) *[wherever it happens to be standing]*
- **Blurb:** A disciplined party with deep roots in Pashtun KP and northern Balochistan, and the most reliable compass in Pakistani politics. It has sat in nearly every government since 1988, it organised the march to remove Imran, and it now opposes the government that march produced. When JUI-Flexi moves, the government is about to change.

### Jamaat-e-Dharna
- **Based on:** Jamaat-e-Islami
- **Symbol:** Scales (tarazu) weighing an electricity bill against public patience. The bill wins.
- **Colours:** Sit-in navy and bill-burning orange. *These are in-game colours; JI's real flag colours are unverified.*
- **Slogan:** "Haq Do Awam Ko" (Give the people their rights) *[and take back the bill]*
- **Blurb:** The most disciplined street force in the country: neat rows of protesters, punctual speeches, a charity ambulance network and zero litter. It won zero National Assembly general seats in 2024. When it was handed one Sindh Assembly seat, its leader gave it back, which the other factions still cannot process.

### The King's Party Franchise™
- **Based on:** Istehkam-e-Pakistan Party (IPP) and Balochistan Awami Party (BAP), with alumni PML-Q (2002) and PTI-Parliamentarians (2023)
- **Symbol:** A folding chair from a press conference, with a private jet parked behind it
- **Colours:** Whatever is in season at Gate No. 4
- **Slogan:** "Press conference se party tak, 48 ghante mein" (From press conference to party in 48 hours)
- **Blurb:**
  - A fast-assembly kit for new parties. Add defectors and a banner and the parts snap together.
  - The 2002 model was built for a general, the 2018 model in Balochistan, and the 2023 model out of post-May-9 press conferences.
  - Each launch is described by commentators as "widely seen as establishment-midwifed".
  - Each is surprised when the voters notice.

### PML-Mitti (PML-Q)
- **Based on:** Pakistan Muslim League (Quaid-e-Azam)
- **Symbol:** A tandem bicycle with two cousins pedalling in opposite directions. *Parody of the historic bicycle; the 2024 symbol is unverified.*
- **Colours:** Gujrat cream and dinner-table gold
- **Slogan:** "Mitti pao" (Bury it and move on)
- **Blurb:** Born in 2002 as a general's party. In 2022 it split between two Gujrat cousins, one of whom went to PTI while the other stayed with PML-N. Every major decision is taken over dinner on Zahoor Elahi Road. It is small and in government, as it always is.

### Tehreek-e-Tafseel-e-Ayeen (TTAP)
- **Based on:** TTAP, the opposition alliance of PkMAP, PTI, MWM and SIC
- **Symbol:** A copy of the Constitution with 400 bookmarks sticking out
- **Colours:** Parchment and ink
- **Slogan:** "Ayeen ki baala-dasti!" (Supremacy of the Constitution!) *[followed by a three-hour footnote]*
- **Blurb:** The Movement to Protect the Constitution, recast as the Movement for the Detailed Recitation of the Constitution. It is led by a veteran nationalist who was once Nawaz's ally and now fronts Imran's opposition. In October 2026 it called off talks with the government, roughly during the preamble.

### Seminar Pakistan (Delisted)
- **Based on:** Awaam Pakistan
- **Symbol:** A PowerPoint clicker
- **Colours:** Think-tank beige
- **Slogan:** "Badlenge nizam" (We'll change the system) *[at the next seminar]*
- **Blurb:** Founded by a former PM and a former finance minister to rescue Pakistan from dynasties. The members' children will not inherit the party, mainly because there isn't much party to inherit yet. The ECP delisted it in 2026, and the party is challenging that, naturally with a seminar.

### Awami One-Man League
- **Based on:** Awami Muslim League (AML)
- **Symbol:** A Gate No. 4 access keycard. *The real pen-and-inkpot symbol is unverified.*
- **Colours:** Lal Haveli red
- **Slogan:** "Qurbani se pehle qurbani" (A sacrifice before the Eid sacrifice)
- **Blurb:** One founder, one constituency and one mansion in Rawalpindi. It won zero seats in 2024. Its prophecies, however, keep coming in at a reliable rate.

### Awami Lantern Party
- **Based on:** Awami National Party (ANP)
- **Symbol:** A lantern (laaltain), the only light still on during load-shedding
- **Colours:** Red
- **Slogan:** "Load-shedding? Hamare paas lantern hai." (Load-shedding? We've got a lantern.)
- **Blurb:** Secular Pashtun nationalists and heirs of the Khudai Khidmatgar movement. In 2024 they won zero National Assembly seats, their worst showing in decades, and they find that more insulting than anything their rivals have said. They are anti-Taliban and anti-operation, and they would like a word about both. *No character in this build; ANP can appear in event cards.*

### The Balochistan Benches
- **Based on:** BNP-Mengal and the National Party
- **Symbol:** A resignation letter in an in-tray, still being processed
- **Colours:** Desert ochre
- **Slogan:** "Intikhabat azaad hon" (Let the elections be free)
- **Blurb:** Nationalists working inside the federation, with a running complaint that Balochistan's elections are engineered in a "lab". In 2024 their leader resigned his National Assembly seat in protest, and it took the system 17 months to accept the resignation. **The joke is on the system, never on them** (see Section 5). *No character in this build.*

### The Establishment (Pindi Holdings Pvt. Ltd.)
- **Based on:** The military high command and intelligence agencies (GHQ, ISI, ISPR)
- **Symbol:** Gate No. 4
- **Colours:** Khaki and olive, with an accent made of every colour on a ribbon rack
- **Slogan:** "Hum neutral hain" (We are neutral) *[terms revised annually]*
- **Supporter unit:** Khalai Makhlooq (the "aliens", which is talk-show code for agency men)
- **Blurb:** The faction that insists it isn't a faction. It ruled directly for about 33 years and has shaped the rest. It isn't on the ballot, but it's in every coalition photo. Every civilian faction has, at some point, been on its Same Page, and every one of them has later been torn out of the book.

### The Robes
- **Based on:** The Supreme Court, the Federal Constitutional Court and the higher judiciary as institutions
- **Symbol:** Two gavels on one block, each pointing at the other
- **Colours:** Black, white and a contested grey
- **Slogan:** "Coordinate, not subordinate"
- **Blurb:**
  - Since the 26th Amendment, Parliament picks the chief justice. Since the 27th, there are two apex courts, two chief justices and one staircase.
  - Two senior judges resigned in protest. In-game they appear as two empty chairs that nobody is allowed to move.
  - Every petition is now first a question of which building it belongs in.

### The Commission
- **Based on:** The Election Commission of Pakistan
- **Symbol:** A cricket bat in an evidence bag, with Form 45 and Form 47 pinned beside it
- **Colours:** Form-pad beige and holdover grey
- **Slogan:** "Nataij thodi der mein" (Results shortly)
- **Blurb:** It runs elections, allots symbols, and decides which party chairmen exist. It took PTI's bat in 2024, delisted a party in 2026, and is led by a commissioner whose term ended in 2025. Every faction is convinced the Commission works for someone else.

### PML-NIHARI

#### 1. Mian Nashtaaz Sharif
- **Based on:** Nawaz Sharif, PML-N president and three-time PM
- **Faction:** PML-Nihari
- **Class:** Summoner (Patriarch). *"Three-time PM. Three-time ex-PM. Unbeaten at breakfast."*

**Bio.** The Lion of Lahore in his elder-statesman years.
- He has been PM three times and was removed before a full term every time, so his trophy cabinet has a permanently empty fourth shelf.
- He was disqualified over an undeclared Dubai iqama. He was convicted in two references and both convictions were later overturned.
- He came back from four years in London, won his Lahore seat, and handed the top job to his younger brother.
- He runs the party like a grandfather runs the dinner table: he rarely speaks, and everyone watches which dish he points at.
- Fuel: siri-paye.

**Voice lines**
- "Mujhe kyun nikala?" (Why was I removed?) *[Plays automatically whenever any character in any faction is disqualified.]*
- "Vote ko izzat do." (Respect the vote.) [Pause.] "Kis ka vote? Woh baad mein batayenge." (Whose vote? We'll let you know later.)
- "Pehle nashta, phir mashwara." (Breakfast first, then the consultation.)
- "Main London se wapas aa gaya hoon. Filhaal." (I'm back from London. For now.)
- "Shehbaz, tum PM ban jao. Main bas dekhta rahunga." (Shehbaz, you be PM. I'll just keep watching.)

**Moves**
- **Signature: GT Road Lament.** When he is disqualified, he drives from Islamabad to Lahore over four turns and gains +2 Sympathy on every GT Road tile.
- **Passive: Siri-Paye Summit.** Any deal struck over breakfast at Jati Umra costs half the usual Political Capital.
- **Weakness: The Five-Year Wall.** At 4 years and 11 months in office, the floor tile under him vanishes.

**Rivals:** Imran U-Khan, Qamar Javed Badalwa, Faiz Chaimeed. **Allies:** Speedbaz Sharif, Maryam Nawazish, Ishaq Dollar.

**Art notes:**
- A large, dignified Lahori patriarch in an oversized shawl and waistcoat, moving with slow, measured hand gestures.
- He holds a breakfast tray of paaye and naan, with a folded London umbrella under his arm.
- A drowsy plush tiger sits at his feet.
- Exaggerate the contented half-smile. No hair or health gags.

#### 2. Speedbaz Sharif
- **Based on:** Shehbaz Sharif, Prime Minister
- **Faction:** PML-Nihari
- **Class:** Berserker. *"Zero to Habib Jalib in three seconds."*

**Bio.** He was Khadim-e-Aala of Punjab, he is PM now, and he runs on adrenaline and revolutionary poetry.
- He vowed to drag Zardari through the streets, then made him President. The game counts that as dragging him all the way to the presidency.
- He offered to sell his own clothes for cheap flour. The wardrobe is still intact.
- He has praised a US president in terms that made the translators blush, and he hosted the Islamabad Talks.
- He stands on the Same Page so firmly that the page has his footprints on it.
- Microphones within arm's reach take fall damage.

**Voice lines**
- "Aise dastoor ko, subh-e-benoor ko, main nahin maanta!" (Such a law, such a lightless dawn, I do not accept!) *[Recited while signing the 27th Amendment.]*
- "Main apne kapray bech doonga, lekin awam ko sasta atta doonga!" (I'll sell my clothes, but I'll give the people cheap flour!)
- "Sarkon par ghaseetenge!" (We'll drag him through the streets!) … "Mubarak ho, Sadr Sahib." (Congratulations, Mr President.)
- "Mr President, you are a man of peace. A man of great peace. A man of peace, and also peace."
- "Hum sab same page par hain. Page number? Woh Pindi batayega." (We're all on the same page. Which page? Pindi will tell us.)

**Moves**
- **Signature: Mic Drop (Literal).** A finger-wagging tirade knocks over every microphone on the stage. Opponents are stunned for one turn and the sound engineer for two.
- **Passive: Shehbaz Speed.** He builds metro, flyover and underpass tiles in half the time, each opened with a ribbon and a hat.
- **Weakness: Elder Brother Override.** Mian Nashtaaz can veto any of his decisions during breakfast. If he steps off the Same Page, he loses 50% of his power.

**Rivals:** Imran U-Khan, Aleema Khabar, Barishwal (a frenemy). **Allies:** Field Marshal Aseem Lamehdood, Mohsin Naka-vi, Asif Ali Bhari (these days).

**Art notes:**
- Caught mid-lunge, with his index finger raised like a sword.
- Waistcoat over a crisp shirt, and the wide-brimmed flood-tour hat.
- A trail of toppled microphones behind him.
- A Habib Jalib paperback in his back pocket and a Nobel-nomination envelope in his hand.

#### 3. Maryam Nawazish Sharif
- **Based on:** Maryam Nawaz, Chief Minister of Punjab
- **Faction:** PML-Nihari
- **Class:** Influencer (Support). *"If it moves in Punjab, it has her photo on it."*

**Bio.** Chief Minister of Punjab and head of the Department of Her Own Face.
- She once ran PML-N's social-media cell and now runs a province the same way. Every ration card, laptop and garbage truck carries her portrait, and every hospital visit arrives pre-edited.
- She survived a trial by typeface: a trust deed dated 2006 was set in Calibri, a font released in 2007. She was later acquitted in the case.
- She led the "vote ko izzat do" campaign, then inherited the province the vote went to.
- She calls Imran "fitna" (mischief). What she calls Bilawal is still being drafted.

**Voice lines**
- "Punjab ki taraf ungli uthayi to ungli tor denge." (Point a finger at Punjab and we'll break the finger.)
- "Is laptop par meri tasveer kyun nahin? Distribution roko." (Why isn't my picture on this laptop? Stop the distribution.)
- "Camera on hai? Theek hai, ab mareez ka haal poochti hoon." (Is the camera on? Good, now I'll ask the patient how he's feeling.)
- "Font? Kaun sa font? Main hamesha se Times New Roman hoon." (Font? What font? I've always been Times New Roman.)
- "Fitna ka ilaaj sirf ek hai: nayi scheme, nayi tasveer." (There's only one cure for fitna: a new scheme with a new photo.)

**Moves**
- **Signature: Branding Blitz.** She stamps her portrait onto every friendly Punjab tile. Each tile gains +Loyalty and the treasury pays for the posters.
- **Passive: Content Creator.** Every action she takes generates a 30-second reel, which raises approval with Gen Z and no one else.
- **Weakness: Calibri Vulnerability.** Opponents can inspect any document she produces. If the font is anachronistic, she is stunned for one turn.

**Rivals:** Barishwal Bhutto Zardari (the flood-aid feud), Imran U-Khan, Aleema Khabar. **Allies:** Mian Nashtaaz Sharif, Speedbaz Sharif (warily).

**Art notes:**
- Poised and permanently camera-ready.
- A ring light on a selfie stick follows her like a halo.
- She carries a giant rubber stamp of her own face.
- A ceremonial police cap hangs on a hat stand nearby, a nod to Police Day.
- A document under her arm has a "Calibri" font tag sticking out.
- No jokes about her clothes or body. The ring light carries the gag.

#### 4. Ishaq Dollar
- **Based on:** Ishaq Dar, Deputy PM and Foreign Minister
- **Faction:** PML-Nihari
- **Class:** Alchemist. *"The rupee is strong. I am holding it up personally."*

**Bio.** A chartered accountant, four-time finance minister, Nawaz's samdhi (his son is married to Nawaz's daughter) and the family's Mr Fix-It.
- His signature theory, "Darnomics", pins the rupee in place by willpower while the open market quietly disagrees.
- In 2017 he was declared an absconder in an assets case and spent five years in London. He came back once the case had gone away and became Deputy PM.
- He now handles US–Iran diplomacy with the same calculator he used on the exchange rate.
- His forecasts are always rosy. The numbers always arrive next quarter.

**Voice lines**
- "Dollar ki asli value 200 hai. Market ko abhi pata nahin chala." (The dollar's real value is 200. The market just hasn't found out yet.)
- "Sab theek ho jayega. Main ne Excel mein check kiya hai." (Everything will be fine. I checked it in Excel.)
- "London? Main ek meeting ke liye gaya tha. Paanch saal ki meeting thi." (London? I went for a meeting. It was a five-year meeting.)

### 5.3 Legal and sensitivity flags for the designer

1. **Pakistani law.** Get a Pakistani media lawyer to review the content before any release that can be reached from Pakistan. The main exposures are:
   - Criminal defamation (PPC ss. 499–500).
   - PECA, as amended in 2025, which adds "false information" offences.
   - Contempt of court, which matters for The Robes and for the Commission, since the ECP has contempt powers.
   - Constitutional and statutory limits on speech about the armed forces and the judiciary.
   - The blasphemy provisions (PPC 295–298), which is why this document contains no religious content at all.
2. **Altaf Hussain.** A 2015 Lahore High Court order bars Pakistani media from broadcasting his speeches and images. Check whether distributing a game in Pakistan, or marketing it there (trailers, social posts), would be caught. If needed, swap in a regional build that shows only his phone.
3. **Names likely to raise flags in review:**
   - "Stamp-Khan" (the rubber-stamp jab). Fallback: "Bench-Khan".
   - "Sikandar Saintaalis Raja" (ties him to the Form 47 dispute). Fallback: "Sikandar Holdover Raja".
   - "Namaloom Malik". Fallback given above.

   Keep the fallbacks ready.
4. **Status changes before ship.** The brief is dated 31 Oct 2026 and several bios rest on fast-moving facts. Recheck all of these before lock:
   - KP governor's rule and Sohail Afridi's custody (ATC hearing 4 Nov 2026; the emergency was ratified for two months).
   - Omar Ayub's whereabouts (unverified for 2026).
   - Qureshi's custody.
   - Imran's appeals.
   - Whether Murad Ali Shah, Kamran Tessori and CEC Sikandar Sultan Raja are still in office (all [unverified] for Oct 2026).
   - Pervaiz Elahi's party role and Salman Akram Raja's post (both [unverified]).
   - Whether Asim Malik's extension happened ([unverified]).

   If anyone named in custody suffers serious harm, pull their sheet.
