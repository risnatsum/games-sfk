# Types

Six types, two triangles:

- **Khaki > Jalsa > Mic > Khaki**
- **Qanoon > Khandaan > Jor-Tor > Qanoon**

Each type also beats one type from the other triangle.

- **Khaki:** the establishment. Type icon is the Same Page, an actual sheet of A4.
- **Qanoon:** courts and the Election Commission.
- **Jor-Tor:** deals and the people who make them.
- **Mic:** anyone with a microphone.
- **Jalsa:** rallies and long marches.
- **Khandaan:** dynasties.

## In a fight

- Specials, Supers and Ultimates carry the character's type. Normals and throws don't.
- A super-effective hit does a bit more damage and gets the callout. A resisted hit does a bit less and gets a shrug.
- **Awaam** is the health bar. **Political Capital** is the super meter.
- Time-out wins are **Form 47** wins. The winner is whoever the screen says it is.
