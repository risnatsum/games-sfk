# Speedbaz

**Based on:** Shehbaz Sharif (internal only)
**Type:** Jor-Tor
**Style:** Rushdown. Gets in your face and stays there, finger first.
**Tagline:** "Zero to Habib Jalib in three seconds."
**Alt tagline:** "Opened ahead of schedule."

Prime Minister. He can build a flyover before you've finished objecting to it. Recites revolutionary poetry at full volume, mostly at microphones, which rarely survive.

## Look
- **Outfit:** Waistcoat, crisp shirt, the wide-brimmed flood-tour hat.
- **Props:** Giant ribbon-cutting scissors. A Habib Jalib paperback in his back pocket. He stands on a sheet of A4, and it follows him.
- **Intro:** Cuts a ribbon. The stage is declared open.
- **Dizzy:** Microphones circling his head.
- **Victory:** Unveils a plaque with his name on it.
- **KO:** Goes down and takes every mic stand with him.
- **Stage:** A Lahore underpass under construction. By the last round it's finished and named after him.

## Audio
- **Voice:** Starts reasonable, ends shouting with a finger in the air. Every sentence lunges.
- **Lines:**
  - "Aise dastoor ko, subh-e-benoor ko, main nahin maanta!" (Such a law, such a lightless dawn, I do not accept!)
  - "Main apne kapray bech doonga!" (I'll sell my clothes!)
  - "Hum sab same page par hain." (We're all on the same page.)
  - "A man of peace. A man of great peace. A man of peace."
  - "Main nahin maanta!" (I don't accept it!) *[on getting hit]*
- **Music:** Brass band at double tempo.
- **Sounds:** Mic stands clattering. Scissors snipping.
- **Announcer:** "SPEEDBAAAZ!" One breath, no pause. Then the mic falls over.

## Moves
- **Main Nahin Maanta** (I don't accept): Chained rush, one finger-wag per line of the poem. He knows all the lines.
- **Mic Drop:** Overhead. He slams the podium and the mics go flying.
- **Overnight Flyover:** A flyover builds itself under him. He runs over projectiles and lands on your other side.
- **Throw: Sarkon Par Ghaseetenge** (We'll drag you through the streets): Command grab. Drags them the length of the screen, then hangs a President's sash on them. "Mubarak ho, Sadr Sahib." (Congratulations, Mr President.)
- **Super: Aise Dastoor Ko.** The full recital. He charges across the screen, a hit for every verse, and every mic on the stage falls over.
- **Ultimate: Orange Line.** Track appears across the screen in a blink. The train comes through. He cuts the ribbon, and the opponent is the first passenger.

## Abilities
- **Passive: Shehbaz Speed.** Fastest dash in the game. No backdash.
- **Weakness: Off the Page.** Knock him off his sheet of A4 and he's just a man in a hat until he finds it again.

## Matchups
- **Weak to:** Khandaan, Mic
- **Strong against:** Qanoon, Jalsa
- **Rivals:**
  - **Imran U-Turn**
    - Imran: "Absolutely not!"
    - Speedbaz: "Absolutely yes! What was the question?"
  - **Field Marshal Aseem Lamehdood**
    - Speedbaz: "Sir, hum same page par hain!" (Sir, we're on the same page!)
    - Lamehdood: *[Turns the page.]*
- **Win quote:** "Agla round? Uska ribbon main pehle hi kaat chuka hoon." (Next round? I've already cut its ribbon.)
