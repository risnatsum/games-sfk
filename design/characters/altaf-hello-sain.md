# Altaf Hello-sain

**Based on:** Altaf Hussain (internal only)
**Type:** Mic
**Style:** Zoner. Fights from London as a hologram.
**Tagline:** "Live from London. Still on line one."

**Look:** Cardigan, big glasses, rotary phone. The cord runs off-screen.
**Voice:** Phone-filtered. Whispers, then shouts, then sings.

**Moves**
- **Hello? Hello?** Sound-ring projectile.
- **Phone-In Address** Starts a speech. The longer it runs, the stronger he gets. Hit him and the line drops.
- **Rehnuma Chahiye** A crowd bursts up from the floor.
- **Super: Ek Gaana Sunata Hoon** He sings. Notes everywhere.
- **Ultimate: Line One from London** The opponent has to stand through a 4-hour speech.

**Rival:** Farooq Ek-Ghanta Sattar
- Farooq: "Bhai, main siyasat chhor raha hoon." (I'm quitting politics.)
- Altaf: "Theek hai. Ek ghante baad call karna." (Fine. Call me back in an hour.)

**Win quote:** "Main band hoon? Nahin bhai, TV band hai." (I'm banned? No, the TV is.)
