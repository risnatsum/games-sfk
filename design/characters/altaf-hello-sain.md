# Altaf Hello-sain

**Based on:** Altaf Hussain (internal only)
**Type:** Mic
**Style:** Zoner. Slow, long range, wants you far away and listening.
**Tagline:** "Live from London. Still on line one."
**Alt tagline:** "Your call is important to him. Please hold."

Founded a party, moved to London, and has run it by phone ever since. He doesn't come to the fight. He's beamed in as a hologram, and the speech goes on until somebody cuts the line.

## Look
- **Outfit:** Cardigan. Huge glasses, huge moustache. A headset mic and a handset, just in case.
- **Props:** Rotary phone. The cord runs off the edge of the screen. Mug of tea in the idle.
- **Intro:** Dial-up noise while the hologram builds itself line by line.
- **Dizzy:** A buffering spinner instead of stars.
- **Victory:** He raises one hand. The crowd goes silent.
- **KO:** "Call Ended" card. Dial tone.
- **Stage:** Karachi rally ground at night, his face on a giant screen. The crowd hasn't moved in hours.

## Audio
- **Voice:** Everything through a phone filter. Goes from a whisper to a shout with no warning. Breaks into song. Long pauses nobody is allowed to fill.
- **Lines:**
  - "Hello? Hello? Saathiyon, awaaz aa rahi hai?" (Comrades, can you hear me?)
  - "Khamoshi! Main bol raha hoon." (Silence! I'm speaking.)
  - "Ab main aap ko ek gaana sunata hoon." (Now I'll sing you a song.)
  - "Record ki zaroorat nahin. Main live hoon." (Who needs a record? I'm live.)
  - "Line mein disturbance hai." (There's trouble on the line.) *[on getting hit]*
- **Music:** Lift-music version of a rally anthem. Full dhol when he's winning.
- **Sounds:** Rotary-dial clicks and international-call pips.
- **Announcer:** "ALTAF… *[phone rings]* …HELLO-SAIN!"

## Moves
- **Hello? Hello?** Sound-ring projectile. Rings like an old phone.
- **Phone-In Address:** Speech stance. Stronger the longer he talks.
- **Rehnuma Chahiye** (We want the leader): A chanting crowd bursts out of the floor. Anti-air.
- **Long-Distance Cord:** The cord lassos the opponent and reels them in.
- **Throw: Please Hold.** Receiver to their ear, hold music. They wander off dizzy.
- **Super: Ek Gaana Sunata Hoon.** He sings. Notes rain down across the whole screen.
- **Ultimate: Line One from London.** "Connecting to London…" A giant receiver drops the opponent into a rally, where they stand and listen. The clock in the corner hits 4h 37m. The crowd's last chant is the KO.

## Abilities
- **Passive: Still Talking.** The speech carries over between rounds. It doesn't stop just because the round did.
- **Weakness: Dropped Call.** Hit him mid-speech and the line drops. Buffering, then back to zero.
