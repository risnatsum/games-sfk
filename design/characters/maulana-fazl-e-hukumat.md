# Maulana Fazl-e-Hukumat

**Based on:** Fazlur Rehman (internal only)
**Type:** Jalsa
**Style:** Grappler. Gets close, puts an arm round you, and you end up on his side.
**Tagline:** "In every government. Except, for now, this one."
**Alt tagline:** "With democracy. Wherever it is."

Has sat in almost every government the country has had. Marched to bring one down, then went into opposition against the one that replaced it. See which way he's facing and you'll know who governs next.

## Look
- **Outfit:** Black waistcoat, shawl over one shoulder.
- **Props:** A weathervane he leans on like a walking stick. A thick ledger of coalitions, still open.
- **Intro:** Checks which way the weathervane is pointing, then walks on from that side.
- **Dizzy:** The weathervane spins.
- **Victory:** Sits down in the best chair on the stage. Nobody saw him take it.
- **KO:** Dusts himself off and goes to stand behind the winner.
- **Stage:** A march camp outside Islamabad. The road sign points both ways.

## Audio
- **Voice:** Warm and unhurried. A chuckle before every sentence and after most of them.
- **Lines:**
  - *[Chuckle]* "Dekhiye…" (Look…)
  - "Hum har haal mein jamhooriyat ke saath hain." (We stand with democracy, whatever happens.)
  - "Establishment mulk chala rahi hai!" (The establishment is running the country!) *[Crowd: "Ab pata chala?" (You've only just noticed?)]*
  - "Azadi March phir karenge. Kis ke khilaaf? Mashwara jaari hai." (We'll march again. Against whom? Still consulting.)
  - "Yeh mashwaray ke baghair hua." (Nobody consulted me on that.) *[on getting hit]*
- **Music:** Marching drums that keep changing direction.
- **Sounds:** A creaking weathervane. A chuckle on every grab.
- **Announcer:** "FAZL-E… *[chuckle]* …HUKUMAT!"

## Moves
- **Kingmaker's Chuckle:** Command grab. An arm round the shoulder, a chuckle, and they're facing the other way.
- **Mashwara** (Consultation): Anti-air grab. Plucks you out of the air and sits you down at the table.
- **Weathervane:** A spin that ends on whichever side of you suits him.
- **Azadi March:** A long running grab. Who it's against gets decided on the way.
- **Super: Har Hukumat Mein** (In every government): A spinning grab, one turn for every coalition he's joined. It's a long animation.
- **Ultimate: Ittehad** (Alliance): Every leader in the game climbs onto one container with him and they drop the opponent together. Then they fall out over the photo.

## Abilities
- **Passive: Kingmaker.** When the round is close, he builds meter off both players' hits.
- **Weakness: Clear Majority.** Build a big lead and he's got nothing to bargain with.
