# Qazi Faisla Isa

**Based on:** Qazi Faez Isa (internal only)
**Type:** Qanoon
**Style:** Counter-fighter. Does nothing until you do something wrong.
**Tagline:** "Took the bat. Kept the receipt."
**Alt tagline:** "Sit down, counsel."

Former Chief Justice. Took a party's bat away weeks before an election and still has it, bagged and tagged. In court he let you talk, then cut you off. He fights the same way.

## Look
- **Outfit:** Black robe, white bands. Not a crease on either.
- **Props:** A cricket bat in a clear evidence bag, tag and all.
- **Intro:** Pulls up in a small car and parks it himself. No escort.
- **Dizzy:** Adjournment slips circling his head.
- **Victory:** Puts something of yours in a bag and tags it.
- **KO:** "RETIRED" stamped across the screen. The bat slides out of its bag.
- **Stage:** Courtroom One, live on every channel. The lawyers in the gallery are still waiting for a date.

## Audio
- **Voice:** Clipped and precise. Interrupts, including your attacks.
- **Lines:**
  - "Qanoon ke mutabiq, bat wapas nahin milega." (By law, the bat is not coming back.)
  - "Intra-party election dikhaiye. Nahin hai? Agla case." (Show me your intra-party election. No? Next case.)
  - "Baith jaiye." (Sit down.)
  - "Aap ka jawab main de deta hoon." (I'll answer that for you.)
  - "Yeh live ja raha hai." (This is going out live.) *[on getting hit]*
- **Music:** One tanpura note, held. A court typewriter keeps time.
- **Sounds:** Rubber stamps. The clerk calling the next case.
- **Announcer:** "Order! Order! …QAZI FAISLA ISA!"

## Moves
- **Objection Overruled.** Parry stance. Catch a hit and answer it.
- **Baith Jaiye** (Sit down): Low sweep. Counsel sits.
- **Agla Case** (Next case): Quick backhand that sends them full screen. He's done with you.
- **Command Grab: Paperwork.** Asks for a document they don't have. They stand there looking for it.
- **Super: Live Telecast.** Cameras roll. A long, precise combo on every channel.
- **Ultimate: Symbol Withdrawn.** He bags their weapon, tags it and hands them a kettle. They finish the round with the kettle.

## Abilities
- **Passive: Evidence Bag.** Every special he parries is confiscated for the rest of the round.
- **Weakness: Nothing Before the Court.** He won't start anything. Don't attack and he has nothing to rule on.

## Matchups
- **Weak to:** Jor-Tor, Jalsa
- **Strong against:** Khandaan, Khaki
- **Rivals:**
  - **Imran U-Khan**
    - Imran: "Mera bat wapas do!" (Give me back my bat!)
    - Qazi: "Aap to bowler the." (You were a bowler.)
  - **Field Marshal Aseem Lamehdood**
    - Aseem: "Retire ho gaye?" (So you retired?)
    - Qazi: "Ji. Kabhi aap bhi try kijiye." (Yes. You should try it sometime.)
- **Win quote:** "Short order: aap haar gaye. Detailed reasons baad mein." (Short order: you lost. Detailed reasons to follow.)
