# Sikandar Saintaalis

**Based on:** Sikandar Sultan Raja (internal only)
**Type:** Qanoon
**Style:** Staller. Gets a lead, then plays for the clock.
**Tagline:** "Term expired. Still here."
**Alt tagline:** "Results shortly."

Chief Election Commissioner. His term ran out a while ago and he stayed on, because nobody has agreed who comes next. He runs the fight the way he runs results night: slowly, with the signal off.

## Look
- **Outfit:** Grey suit, grey tie. A grey file under one arm.
- **Props:** Form 45 in one hand, Form 47 in the other. They don't line up.
- **Intro:** A "Mobile service suspended" banner scrolls past. He's already standing there.
- **Dizzy:** Unofficial results circling his head.
- **Victory:** Victory banners drop for both players. He's already left.
- **KO:** He goes down. The scoreboard says "RESULTS PENDING".
- **Stage:** Election Commission back office at night. Ballot boxes to the ceiling. A cricket bat in an evidence bag on the shelf.

## Audio
- **Voice:** Flat and quiet. Every line sounds like a press note.
- **Lines:**
  - "Nataij thodi der mein." (Results shortly.)
  - "Form 45, Form 47, dono forms hain. Masla kya hai?" (They're both forms. What's the problem?)
  - "Mera term khatam ho gaya. Main nahin hua." (My term ended. I didn't.)
  - "Aap chairman nahin hain. Hum ne check kiya." (You're not the chairman. We checked.)
  - "Shikayat? Form bhar dein." (A complaint? Fill in a form.) *[on getting hit]*
- **Music:** A ticking clock over a bored synth. The tempo drops when he's winning.
- **Sounds:** Stamps. A fax machine that never finishes.
- **Announcer:** "SIKANDAR… *[47 seconds of silence]* …SAINTAALIS!"

## Moves
- **Ballot Box.** Sets a sealed box down between you. A wall you have to break through.
- **Signal Suspended.** Your health and meter go blank for a while. For security.
- **Tasleem Nahin** (Not recognised): A stamp to the face. Their next combo doesn't count.
- **Throw: Contempt Notice.** Ask him to resign and find out.
- **Super: Form 45, Form 47.** Two giant forms slam in from either side. They don't line up, and you're in the gap.
- **Ultimate: Nataij Thodi Der Mein.** The lights go out and the hits keep landing in the dark. The clock spins through the night and most of the next day. Then the scoreboard updates.

## Abilities
- **Passive: Holdover.** Empty his health bar and he stays on anyway, at a sliver. It takes one more clean hit to replace him.
- **Weakness: Live Results.** Rush him. Up close there's nothing to delay.

## Matchups
- **Weak to:** Jor-Tor, Jalsa
- **Strong against:** Khandaan, Khaki
- **Rivals:**
  - **Imran U-Turn**
    - Imran: "Istifa do!" (Resign!)
    - Sikandar: "Notice mil jayega." (You'll be getting a notice.)
  - **Speedbaz**
    - Speedbaz: "Shukriya, Sikandar sahib!" (Thank you, Sikandar sahib!)

- **Win quote:** "Mubarak ho. Aap bhi jeet gaye." (Congratulations. You won too.)
