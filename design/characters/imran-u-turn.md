# Imran U-Turn

**Based on:** Imran Khan (internal only)
**Type:** Jalsa
**Style:** Comeback. Ordinary when he's ahead, frightening when cornered.
**Tagline:** "Absolutely not. Absolutely… let's see."
**Alt tagline:** "This is the final call. Until the next one."

Won a World Cup, then an election. He says a leader who never U-turns is no leader at all. He is a very great leader. Every march is the final one.

## Look
- **Outfit:** Crisp shalwar kameez, Peshawari chappals. Sleeves rolled up like he's about to bowl.
- **Props:** A cricket bat in an evidence bag marked "CONFISCATED". A rolled-up letter he never lets anyone read.
- **Intro:** Walks out to bat, U-turns halfway, then walks out again.
- **Dizzy:** A tiny steering wheel spinning round his head.
- **Victory:** Lifts the trophy on his own. The team is somewhere off-screen.
- **KO:** The umpire's finger goes up. He asks for a review.
- **Stage:** D-Chowk, from the top of a container. Shipping containers wall off both edges of the screen.

## Audio
- **Voice:** Calm, almost chatty, then a full rally roar with no warm-up.
- **Lines:**
  - "Absolutely not!"
  - "Ghabrana nahi hai." (Don't panic.)
  - "Neutral to janwar hota hai." (Only an animal stays neutral.)
  - "Jo leader U-turn nahin leta, woh leader hi nahin." (A leader who never U-turns is no leader.)
  - "Yeh saazish hai!" (It's a conspiracy!) *[waves the letter, on getting hit]*
- **Music:** Stadium anthem. A DJ drops the beat every time he lands a combo.
- **Sounds:** Leather on willow. A crowd that goes "Ooooh" on every near miss.
- **Announcer:** "IMRAN… U… *[tyres screech]* …TURN!"

## Moves
- **Bouncer:** Long run-up, then a fast ball at head height.
- **U-Turn:** Dashes straight past you, handbrake turn, hits you from behind.
- **Absolutely Not!** Counter. Refuses one attack, any attack.
- **Long March:** Walks forward through hits. Only the containers stop him.
- **Throw: Cipher.** Waves the letter in their face. Nobody gets to read it.
- **Super: Final Call.** A rally charges the whole screen. When it ends, the button lights up again: "Final Call 2."
- **Ultimate: Tsunami.** A wave of supporters rolls across the screen and carries the opponent away. The stage comes back as Naya Pakistan. Same stage, new sign.

## Abilities
- **Passive: Cornered Tigers.** The less Awaam he has left, the harder he hits.
- **Weakness: Only Animals Are Neutral.** He can't walk backwards. Be patient and he'll walk into everything.
