# Mohsin Naka-vi

**Based on:** Mohsin Naqvi (internal only)
**Type:** Khaki
**Style:** Turtle. Walls off the screen and makes you come to him.
**Tagline:** "All roads closed."
**Alt tagline:** "Still has the trophy."

Interior Minister and cricket chairman at the same time. He seals the capital with shipping containers in the morning and loses a home series by the evening.

## Look
- **Outfit:** Sharp suit. Cricket pads over the formal trousers.
- **Props:** A gold trophy he hugs to his chest. A walkie-talkie. A shipping container slung over his shoulder like a bag.
- **Intro:** A container drops. He steps out from behind it, trophy first.
- **Dizzy:** Tiny containers circling his head.
- **Victory:** Holds the trophy out. Nobody comes to take it. He takes it home.
- **KO:** Down, but still holding the trophy.
- **Stage:** D-Chowk, Islamabad, every road sealed with containers. A scoreboard on the Parliament lawn shows Pakistan losing.

## Audio
- **Voice:** Smooth and unhurried. Talks into the walkie-talkie mid-combo.
- **Lines:**
  - "Container lagao. Haan, wahan bhi." (Put a container there. Yes, there too.)
  - "Trophy chahiye? Mere daftar aa jao." (Want the trophy? Come by my office.)
  - "Team ki performance? Main board se poochta hoon. Ek minute… woh bhi main hoon." (The team's form? Let me ask the board. One minute… that's me too.)
  - "Main appointed hoon. Fark samjho." (I'm appointed. Learn the difference.)
  - "Inquiry hogi." (There'll be an inquiry.) *[on getting hit]*
- **Music:** A brass band, interrupted by cricket highlights.
- **Sounds:** Containers landing with a clang. Walkie-talkie static. Bat on ball.
- **Announcer:** "MOHSIN NAKA—" *[whistle]* The announcer gets waved through. "—VI!"

## Moves
- **Container Lagao:** Drops a container. Nothing gets through, projectiles included.
- **Naka** (Checkpoint): Counter stance. A barrier arm drops. Run into it and you're asked for ID.
- **Section 144:** No gatherings. Marks a zone the opponent can't dash in.
- **Over the Wall:** Containers stack up under anyone jumping in. Anti-air.
- **Throw: Trophy Ceremony.** Holds out the trophy. They reach for it. He walks off with it.
- **Super: Container Maze.** Containers fill the screen. He's the only one who knows the way through.
- **Ultimate: Chairman's XI.** Stadium floodlights. He bowls, then umpires his own appeal. Out. The scoreboard says Pakistan lost anyway.

## Abilities
- **Passive: Appointed.** Starts every round with a full super meter. Nobody voted on it.
- **Weakness: Own Roadblock.** His containers block him too. Trap him behind his own wall and he can't get out.
