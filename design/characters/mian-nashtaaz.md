# Mian Nashtaaz

**Based on:** Nawaz Sharif (internal only)
**Type:** Khandaan
**Style:** Summoner. Barely gets up from breakfast. The lion and the family do the fighting.
**Tagline:** "Three-time PM. Three-time ex-PM."
**Alt tagline:** "Unbeaten at breakfast."

Came back from London, handed the top job to his younger brother, and sat down to eat. He hardly speaks. Everyone watches which dish he points at.

## Look
- **Outfit:** Shawl and waistcoat. Never in a hurry.
- **Props:** A breakfast tray of paaye and naan. A sleepy lion at his feet.
- **Intro:** Comes down the steps of a London flight and sits straight down to breakfast.
- **Dizzy:** A tiny lion circling his head, yawning.
- **Victory:** Says nothing. Points at one dish. The whole family nods.
- **KO:** Slow piano. The lion lies down.
- **Stage:** A Lahore courtyard laid for breakfast. The family portrait on the wall keeps adding faces.

## Audio
- **Voice:** Slow and measured, long pauses. When he does raise it, it's a question.
- **Lines:**
  - "Vote ko izzat do." (Respect the vote.)
  - "Pehle nashta, phir mashwara." (Breakfast first, then talks.)
  - "Main London se wapas aa gaya hoon. Filhaal." (I'm back from London. For now.)
  - "Mujhe kyun maara?" (Why did you hit me?) *[on getting hit]*
  - "Mujhe kyun nikala?" (Why did they throw me out?) *[on KO]*
- **Music:** A Lahori brass band, playing at breakfast tempo.
- **Sounds:** Clinking plates. A lion that yawns instead of roaring.
- **Announcer:** "SHER AYA… *[yawn]* …MIAN NASHTAAZ!"

## Moves
- **Sher Aya** (The lion's here): Sends the lion across the screen. It's sleepy, but it gets there.
- **Chhote, Tum Karo** (You do it, little brother): Speedbaz runs in, does the fast stuff, runs out.
- **Motorway:** A slab of motorway shoots up at an angle. Anti-air. Built overnight.
- **London:** Leaves the screen. Comes back when it's safe.
- **Super: Tum PM Ban Jao** (You be PM): Speedbaz takes over and fights flat out. Mian Sahib watches from the table.
- **Ultimate: Teesri Baar** (Third Time): He's sworn in three times, and every swearing-in is a huge hit. None of them lasts.

## Abilities
- **Passive: Pehle Nashta** (Breakfast first). Every round starts with him seated. While he's at the table, the family arrives faster.
- **Weakness: Full Term.** He can't win on time. Something always comes up with seconds to go.

## Matchups
- **Weak to:** Qanoon, Khaki
- **Strong against:** Jor-Tor, Mic
- **Rivals:**
  - **Imran U-Turn**
    - Imran: "Kursi chhoro!" (Get out of the chair!)
    - Nashtaaz: "Kaun si? Main to nashte par baitha hoon." (Which one? I'm only sitting at breakfast.)
  - **Speedbaz**
    - Speedbaz: "Bhai jaan, ab main kya karoon?" (Brother, what do I do now?)
    - Nashtaaz: "Pehle nashta." (Breakfast first.)
- **Win quote:** "Dekha? Is baar kisi ne nahin nikala." (See? Nobody threw me out this time.)
