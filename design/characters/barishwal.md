# Barishwal

**Based on:** Bilawal Bhutto Zardari (internal only)
**Type:** Khandaan
**Style:** Stage control. Makes it rain, then lets the water do the work.
**Tagline:** "Forecast: rain. Then water."
**Alt tagline:** "PM candidate. Again."

Party chairman since university and prime-ministerial candidate ever since. He isn't in the government, but it can't survive without his votes, and he'd like you to know. His explanation of the monsoon is still the clearest climate policy in the country.

## Look
- **Outfit:** Sharp blazer, ajrak scarf.
- **Props:** An umbrella that leaks on him. A "PM Candidate" badge.
- **Intro:** Thunder. He opens the umbrella and it starts raining underneath.
- **Dizzy:** A tiny raincloud over his head.
- **Victory:** Crosses out the year on his badge and writes in the next one.
- **KO:** The umbrella blows inside out.
- **Stage:** A flooded Karachi street in the monsoon. Rickshaws float past in the background.

## Audio
- **Voice:** Bright and earnest, always mid-speech. Explains the weather like it's breaking news.
- **Lines:**
  - "Jab barish aata hai, to pani aata hai." (When rain comes, water comes.)
  - "Jab zyada barish aata hai, to zyada pani aata hai." (When more rain comes, more water comes.) *[Super]*
  - "Selected!"
  - "Hamare vote ke baghair yeh hukumat nahin chal sakti." (This government can't last without our votes.) *[Votes for it.]*
  - "Main walk out kar raha hoon." *[Comes straight back.]*
- **Music:** A rally anthem with a thunderclap on the beat.
- **Sounds:** Rain on an umbrella. Squelching shoes.
- **Announcer:** "BARISH… *[thunderclap]* …WAL!"

## Moves
- **Barish Aata Hai** (Rain comes): A cloud drifts over the opponent and rains on them.
- **Selected!** Boomerang. Watch it on the way back.
- **Chhatri** (Umbrella): Anti-air. It leaks.
- **Walkout:** Storms off towards the edge of the screen. Comes straight back.
- **Super: Zyada Pani** (More water): A wave across the whole screen.
- **Ultimate: Zinda Hai** (Lives on): A PPP rally with giant portraits floods the stage and carries the opponent off. On a Punjab stage, it's three men and a banner.

## Abilities
- **Passive: More Rain, More Water.** Every rain move raises the water on the stage. The deeper it gets, the slower the opponent moves.
- **Weakness: Knee-Deep.** Flood it all the way and he's wading too.

## Matchups
