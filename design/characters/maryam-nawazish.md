# Maryam Nawazish

**Based on:** Maryam Nawaz (internal only)
**Type:** Mic
**Style:** Puppet. She fights alongside her camera drone and is never out of shot.
**Tagline:** "If it moves in Punjab, it has her photo on it."
**Alt tagline:** "Set in Calibri since 2006."

Chief Minister of Punjab and head of the Department of Her Own Face. She used to run the party's social-media cell. Now she runs a province like one.

## Look
- **Outfit:** On brand. A lanyard that says MEDIA CELL.
- **Props:** A ring light on a selfie stick. A giant rubber stamp of her own face. A camera drone that never stops filming.
- **Intro:** She walks in. Then she walks in again, because the first take was no good.
- **Dizzy:** Notification pings circling her head.
- **Victory:** Stamps her face on the camera lens.
- **KO:** She goes down. The drone keeps filming. It'll be edited out.
- **Stage:** A Lahore street where everything has her face on it. By round two, so does your health bar.

## Audio
- **Voice:** Crisp and short. Every line is delivered to the camera, not the opponent.
- **Lines:**
  - "Camera on hai?" (Is the camera on?)
  - "Is laptop par meri tasveer kyun nahin?" (Why isn't my picture on this laptop?)
  - "Punjab ki taraf ungli uthayi to ungli tor denge." (Point a finger at Punjab and we'll break the finger.)
  - "Fitna!" (Troublemaker!)
  - "Font? Kaun sa font?" (What font?) *[on getting hit]*
- **Music:** Whatever sound is trending this week. Changes every patch.
- **Sounds:** Camera shutters. A rubber stamp thudding down.
- **Announcer:** Reads her name off a card she wrote for him. Her name is in bigger type than his.

## Moves
- **Camera On Hai?:** Parks the drone on screen. Her specials hit harder while she's in shot.
- **Laptop Scheme:** Throws a laptop with her face on the lid. Projectile.
- **#Fitna:** A hashtag shoots straight up and trends. Anti-air.
- **Branding:** Stamps her face on the opponent. The drone follows them.
- **Super: Suthra Punjab** (Clean Punjab): A fleet of garbage trucks with her portrait on the side sweeps across the screen.
- **Ultimate: Retake.** A film crew rolls in. She lands the hit, then they go again from a better angle. And again. Her face is the thumbnail.

## Abilities
- **Passive: Content.** Posing for the drone builds meter. Her taunt is a real move.
- **Weakness: Calibri.** Throw her and her paperwork spills out. Wrong font. She's dizzy till it's fixed.

## Matchups
- **Weak to:** Jalsa, Khandaan
- **Strong against:** Khaki, Jor-Tor
