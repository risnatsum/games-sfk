# Asif Ali Bhari

**Based on:** Asif Ali Zardari (internal only)
**Type:** Jor-Tor
**Style:** Setplay. Patient, sets traps, lets you walk into them.
**Tagline:** "Always playing both colours."
**Alt tagline:** "He's already read the deal."

Twice President and grandmaster of the back room. He has made a deal with everyone in this game, including the people trying to beat him. He'll make one with you too.

## Look
- **Outfit:** Dark suit. Sunglasses, indoors, always.
- **Props:** A chessboard under his arm, pieces of both colours in his pockets. A presidential pen the size of his forearm.
- **Intro:** Sets the board down and makes a move. Nobody's sitting opposite. He smiles at you anyway.
- **Dizzy:** Chess pieces circling his head.
- **Victory:** A document lands on his desk. He signs it without reading it.
- **KO:** Knocks over his own king. Still smiling.
- **Stage:** A state room in the President's House. A pile of papers waiting for his signature grows all match.

## Audio
- **Voice:** Soft and amused. Never raises it. Laughs at jokes nobody made.
- **Lines:**
  - "Ek Bhari, sab pe bhari!" (One Bhari outweighs them all!)
  - "Signature kahan karoon?" (Where do I sign?)
  - "Parhne ki zaroorat nahin. Deal main pehle parh chuka hoon." (No need to read it. I've already read the deal.)
  - "Sab se baat hogi." (We'll talk to everyone.)
  - "Achha. Chalo, baat karte hain." (All right. Let's talk.) *[on getting hit]*
- **Music:** Slow lounge jazz with a Sindhi lilt.
- **Sounds:** Chess pieces clicking onto the board. A fountain pen scratching.
- **Announcer:** "ASIF ALI BHARI…" *[crowd]* "…SAB PE BHARI!"

## Moves
- **Political Chess:** Puts a chess piece on the floor. Walk into it and it takes you.
- **Eent Se Eent** (Brick by brick): Throws a brick, then he's at the far end of the screen holding a boarding pass.
- **Ek Bhari:** Counter stance. Takes your hit and hands it back, smiling.
- **Throw: Coalition Handshake.** A long handshake for the cameras. When it ends, you're on his side of the screen.
- **Super: Checkmate.** The board unfolds under the opponent. Every piece he's placed moves at once.
- **Ultimate: Sab Pe Bhari.** Everyone he's ever fought walks on and shakes his hand. Then they turn around. The opponent is alone on screen.

## Abilities
- **Passive: Both Sides.** He blocks from either side. Crossups don't work on him.
- **Weakness: Out of Office.** Eent Se Eent has a long landing. He's in Dubai for a bit. Hit him there.

## Matchups
- **Weak to:** Khandaan, Mic
- **Strong against:** Qanoon, Jalsa

  - **Sikandar Saintaalis**
    - Sikandar: "Result abhi aana baaqi hai." (The result isn't in yet.)
    - Bhari: "Mere paas aa gaya hai." (I've got it already.)
