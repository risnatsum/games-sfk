# Field Marshal Aseem Lamehdood

**Based on:** Asim Munir (internal only)
**Type:** Khaki
**Style:** Armoured grappler. Walks through your hits and owns the floor.
**Tagline:** "Rank: Field Marshal. Term: Yes."
**Alt tagline:** "Neutral. Terms revised annually."

Collects titles. The latest one is for life. He's the last fight in arcade mode, and he brought his own stage. Making him smile is a hidden achievement. Nobody has it.

## Look
- **Outfit:** Uniform, peaked cap, moustache. The ribbon rack grows a row every round.
- **Props:** A field marshal's baton tagged "LIFETIME WARRANTY".
- **Intro:** A statue is unveiled. It's him. He steps down off the plinth.
- **Dizzy:** Not authorised.
- **Victory:** He doesn't smile. The billboard behind him does.
- **KO:** He goes down. A banner: "RANK RETAINED."
- **Stage:** A marble plaza of statues, all of him. A new one goes up between rounds.

## Audio
- **Voice:** Low and level. Never hurries. Doesn't find any of this funny.
- **Lines:**
  - "Hum neutral hain." (We are neutral.) *[The whole floor turns khaki.]*
  - "Ayeen parha hai. Naya page khaas taur par achha hai." (I've read the Constitution. The new page is particularly good.)
  - "Muskurahat? Negative." (A smile? Negative.)
  - "Lunch achha tha. Unhon ne favourite kaha." (Lunch was good. He called me his favourite.)
  - "Yeh record mein nahin jayega." (That's off the record.) *[on getting hit]*
- **Music:** A brass march. Each phase adds more brass.
- **Sounds:** Boots on marble. The ribbon rack jingling.
- **Announcer:** "FIELD MARSHAL… ASEEM… LAMEHDOOD!" *[The announcer stands up for this one.]*

## Moves
- **Same Page.** Lays a sheet of A4 on the floor. Step on it and he pulls it out from under you.
- **Hum Neutral Hain** (We are neutral): Armour stance. He's not involved. Your hits land and nothing happens.
- **Billboard.** His face drops from the sky on a billboard. Overhead.
- **Command Grab: Photo-Op.** An arm round your shoulder for the coalition photo. He doesn't let go.
- **Super: Unveiling.** A statue of himself lands on you. Ribbon-cutting, polite applause.
- **Ultimate: Amendment.** The screen goes dark in the middle of the night. When it comes back, the timer reads "LIFETIME" and you're on the floor.

## Abilities
- **Passive: Lifetime Rank.** Can't be knocked down. He can be hit, just not removed.
- **Weakness: Torn Page.** Hit the page, not him. Tear it and the armour comes off.

## Boss
- **Phase 1:** Fights from the Same Page. Armoured while he's on it.
- **Phase 2:** Tear it and he prints another. This one follows him. Wherever he stands is the Same Page.
- **Phase 3:** He passes an amendment. The navy's and air force's health bars join his.
- **Time-out:** Goes to him. The screen says so.
